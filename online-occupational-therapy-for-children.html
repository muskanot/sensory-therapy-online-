Online Occupational Therapy for Children in India

Many children face challenges with attention, sensory processing, motor skills, and daily activities. Online occupational therapy provides structured, evidence-based support from the comfort of your home, guided by a qualified pediatric occupational therapist.

Our online occupational therapy sessions are designed to help children become more independent, confident, and functional in their everyday life while actively involving parents in the therapy process.

Who Needs Online Occupational Therapy?

  • Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)
  • Children with ADHD or attention difficulties
  • Sensory processing challenges
  • Fine motor and handwriting difficulties
  • Delayed daily living skills
  • Emotional regulation and behavior challenges

How Our Online Occupational Therapy Sessions Work

  • Initial online assessment and parent discussion
  • Individualized goal-based therapy plan
  • One-to-one live online OT sessions
  • Parent guidance and home activity programs
  • Regular progress tracking and feedback

Benefits of Online Occupational Therapy for Children

  • Therapy in a familiar and comfortable home environment
  • Active parent involvement in every session
  • Flexible scheduling without travel stress
  • Personalized therapy plans for each child
  • Consistent progress through home-based practice

  • Ethical, realistic, and goal-oriented therapy
