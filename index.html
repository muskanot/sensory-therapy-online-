Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Online Pediatric OT?

Everything you need to know about preparing for your video consultation, our neuroscience-backed methods, and home equipment.

Can pediatric occupational therapy truly be effective online over video? ▼

Yes, studies show tele-occupational therapy achieves equal or higher parental adherence compared to in-clinic therapy. Children are relaxed in their natural environment without clinic fear, and parents learn the direct handling techniques to support their child 24/7.

Do I need special sensory therapy equipment at home? ▼

Not at all! We intentionally use everyday household objects—pillows, sofa cushions, bedsheets for vestibular swings, heavy laundry baskets for proprioceptive work, and kitchen spices/pulses for tactile exploration.

How do I book a consultation and pay? ▼

Simply fill out our official Google Booking Form or message Dr. Muskan on WhatsApp at +91 9810765977. We will confirm your preferred time slot and share UPI / NetBanking payment details.

What if my child refuses to look at the screen or run away? ▼

We never force a child to stare at a screen! Tele-OT is parent-guided. Dr. Muskan watches your child play naturally in the background and instructs you in real time on how to position, engage, and modulate the activity.
